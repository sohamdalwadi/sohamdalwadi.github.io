### 🧠 BrainDrain

An open-source, privacy-first Android application designed to help users take back control over digital distractions and mindless short-form video scrolling.

#### Key Highlights
- **100% Privacy-First**: Zero external trackers, zero cloud dependencies, and zero subscriptions.
- **Native Android & Kotlin**: Built using clean architecture, Jetpack Compose, and background accessibility monitors.
- **Intervention Engine**: Detects repetitive scrolling habits and prompts gentle, configurable friction.
