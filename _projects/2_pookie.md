### ❤️ Pookie

A room-based companion mini-game app built with native Kotlin and Android UI. Designed for couples to play turn-based mini-games and engage in conversational prompts.

#### Key Highlights
- **Room-Based Networking**: Real-time room matchmaking and synchronized game state.
- **Modern Android Stack**: Kotlin, Coroutines, StateFlow, and modern Material design components.
